# Hi, I'm Xingcheng Ni 👋

**PhD Student in Computational Biology · Statistics Enthusiast · Occasionally Writes Too Much LaTeX**

I am currently a PhD student at the **Shanghai Institute of Nutrition and Health, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences**.

My work lies at the intersection of **computational biology and statistics**. I am particularly interested in understanding complex biological data through interpretable statistical models rather than treating machine learning as a black box.

Beyond biology, I spend a perhaps unreasonable amount of time studying probability, statistics, optimization, and the mathematical foundations behind algorithms.

---

## 📚 Selected Projects

### 📐 [math](https://github.com/Expectorpatro/math)

My attempt to reconstruct and organize the mathematics and statistics I have learned over the years.

Rather than simply collecting lecture notes, I try to fill in omitted derivations, reorganize arguments, and understand why results are formulated the way they are.

It has gradually become a fairly large personal knowledge base written in LaTeX.

---

### 🧬 [MLAS](https://github.com/Expectorpatro/MLAS)

Code associated with my first published paper in **Horticulturae**.

The project uses computational approaches to identify genes in **Chinese cabbage** potentially associated with responses to abiotic stresses including cold, heat, salt, and drought.

This was one of my first attempts to connect computation with biological questions.

---

### 🤖 [BERT](https://github.com/Expectorpatro/BERT)

A BERT implementation built from scratch with **PyTorch**, currently focusing on the **Masked Language Modeling** objective.

I built it mainly because implementing an algorithm is often the fastest way for me to understand what is actually happening inside it.

---

### 📊 [sampling-method](https://github.com/Expectorpatro/sampling-method)

A set of notes and implementations on **survey sampling**.

The project started from coursework and gradually evolved into a more systematic collection of derivations, explanations, and practical code.

---

### 📈 [stock](https://github.com/Expectorpatro/stock)

A small project exploring quantitative ideas related to financial markets.

It started as an experiment with a friend and remains very much a work in progress.

No promises of becoming stock-market moguls yet.
